Paper-only cycle tilt (proposed policy clause) and honest limits

Cycle-phase tilt (paper only). When it reads early-cycle (value >= +0.34) the High-risk sleeve may use the upper end of its band and the Leveraged sleeve is permitted within its hard limit. Mid-cycle uses band midpoints. Never overrides hard limits, drawdown stops or per-position caps; re-evaluated monthly; paper portfolios only, never real money.

  • few samples (~4 cycles since 1990)

  • a composite of correlated indicators is not independent evidence
