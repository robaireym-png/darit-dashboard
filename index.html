Paper-only cycle tilt (proposed policy clause) and honest limits

Cycle-phase tilt (paper only). When the Cycle Context composite reads late-cycle (value <= -0.34) the High-risk sleeve target is capped at the low end of its policy band and the Leveraged sleeve is blocked from new positions. When it reads early-cycle (value >= +0.34) the High-risk sleeve may use the upper end of its band and the Leveraged sleeve is permitted within its hard limit. Mid-cycle uses band midpoints. Never overrides hard limits, drawdown stops or per-position caps; re-evaluated monthly; paper portfolios only, never real money.

  • few samples (~4 cycles since 1990)
  • dating chosen in hindsight; NBER dates recessions, not market peaks
  • the 2022-24 inversion ended without a recession, breaking the usual sequence
  • valuation indicators have read 'late-cycle' for most of a decade
  • several historical peak/trough values are approximate representative readings
  • today's Fed, CPI and margin-debt readings rely partly on press reports and secondary sources
  • a composite of correlated indicators is not independent evidence
  • cycle lengths (7-11 yr) have no predictive precision; the 2001-07 cycle ran 6 years
